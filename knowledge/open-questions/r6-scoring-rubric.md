# What is the R6 scoring rubric

[R6](/rules/r6-rank-findings.md) needs a concrete scale for each criterion, a
combining function, and the band that interrupts a human. The paper's example
score of 13 implies a numeric sum. Deferred with the findings queue; v0 reports
may carry an optional `score`.
